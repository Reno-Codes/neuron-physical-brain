# Experiment 59 — OPA189 + 8s×8p frozen F40 vectors

Experiments 1–58 were not modified. Isolated catalog o2. Level-1 TIA +
Level-4 tiled `xhigh_po`. Not silicon. No resistor fitting.

**Status: DEMONSTRATED**
**Decision A — OPA189 + 8s8p PASSES THE FROZEN F40 VECTOR SET (ISOLATED)**
**Realism: Level 1 TIA + Level 4 PDK resistors. Mixed-level isolated cell.**

## 1. Objective

Does the Experiment 58 leftover-limited OPA189 + W=10 8s×8p cell stay
inside 1 mV on the established frozen F40 vector set (19 / 20 / 38 / 6)?

## 2. Baseline

Experiment 58 vec 19 y = 2.460173765013 repeated with Δy = **0**.

## 3. Hypothesis

H1: vec 19 remains the leftover worst case (~0.684 mV) and still passes.
H2: smaller |z| vectors have much smaller leftover (Exp57 scale sweep)
and pass easily.
H3: OPA189 does not add a new vector-dependent millivolt error.

## 4. Method

Same fabric and solver policy as Experiment 58: `ngbehavior=ps`,
official R `.include`, OPA189 ±15 V, W=10 8s×8p, isolated o2.
`select_vectors` from Experiment 43 (frozen F40 `exp24_F`).

## 5. Controls

Vec 19 exact Exp58 repeat.

## 6. Results

| vector | role | z (V) | y (V) | \|err\| (mV) | pass |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 19 | max \|z\| | +2.459490 | 2.460174 | 0.684 | True |
| 20 | most negative z | −0.918912 | −0.918484 | 0.428 | True |
| 38 | mid \|z\| | +0.980937 | 0.980918 | 0.019 | True |
| 6 | lower \|z\| | +0.133713 | 0.133753 | 0.039 | True |

All four pass. Worst is vec 19 leftover, as predicted.

## 7. Mechanism

Leftover / voltco scales with swing. Mid and low |z| residuals are
tens of µV. Negative-z vec 20 is 0.428 mV — same order as Exp46 W=10
OPA189 on that vector (0.431 mV).

## 8. Failure analysis

No failures. Isolated cell only. Matching not re-tested here.

## 9. Promotion decision

set. Not full Network F. Not PDK MOS TIA. Not silicon. Not yield.
