# Experiment 56 — W=20 8s×8p under tt_mm

Experiments 1–55 were not modified. Behavioral TIA A=1e6. Official
SKY130 `tt_mm` AGAUSS, not foundry yield. No resistor fitting.

**Status: PARTIALLY VALIDATED**
**Decision B — W=20 8s8p 15/16; LEFTOVER FLOOR LIKELY DOMINATES (NOT YIELD)**
**Realism: Level 4 PDK resistors + Level 0 behavioral TIA. Not silicon.**

## 1. Objective

Does adding documented width (W=20) to the Experiment 55 8s×8p array
clear the last high-side `tt_mm` tail, or is the +0.48 mV leftover now
the limiter?

## 2. Baseline

Experiment 55 W=10 8s8p nominal y = 2.459968859764 repeated exactly.
That sample was 15/16, mean 0.530 mV, max 1.360 mV, leftover +0.479 mV.

## 3. Hypothesis

H1: W=20 8s8p (256× vs W=10 single, same 5120 instances) halves the
remaining random tail and all-passes this n=16 sample.
H2: leftover stays +0.48 mV; every draw sits above z; the bar is
one-sided; a single high-side tail can still miss.
H3: mean |err| collapses onto leftover (random σ << leftover).

## 4. Method

Same frozen o2 / vec 19 / A=1e6 / official tt and tt_mm.
W=20 same-family encode, then 8s×8p identical units. `set seed=56000+draw`.

## 5. Controls

- W=10 8s8p nominal (Exp55 repeat).
- Signed-error census on the new sample.

## 6. Results

### Nominal leftover

| config | y (V) | \|err\| (mV) | devices |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| w10_8s8p | 2.459968859764 | 0.479289 | 5120 |
| w20_8s8p | 2.459968223476 | 0.478653 | 5120 |

### tt_mm W=20 8s8p (n=16, not yield)

| metric | value |
| --- | ---: |
| pass | 15/16 |
| mean \|err\| | 0.470 mV |
| median \|err\| | 0.401 mV |
| max \|err\| | 1.015 mV |
| std | 0.228 mV |
| signed n_pos / n_neg | 16 / 0 |
| mean signed | +0.470 mV |

The single miss (draw 15) is **+1.015 mV** — 15 µV over the bar.

## 7. Mechanism

H1 is false for all-pass; H2 and H3 are supported. Mean |err| = leftover.
Every draw is above z. Random σ fell from 0.355 mV (W=10 8s8p) to
0.228 mV. The remaining fail is leftover plus a modest high-side tail.

Further area can shave 15 µV. It cannot remove the +0.48 mV floor.

## 8. Failure analysis

Not numerical (all 16 converged). Not a yield claim. The miss is
15 µV; n=16 cannot decide whether a larger sample is 14/16 or 16/16.
The physical claim is: **matching is now smaller than leftover**.

## 9. Promotion decision

**B — PARTIALLY VALIDATED.** 256× documented area puts mean error on
the leftover floor. Sample still 15/16. Not yield. Not silicon.
Physical TIA **NO**. Miller family **CLOSED**.

Do **not** keep enlarging arrays as the primary path.

## 10. Claim map

- **Demonstrated:** Exp55 8s8p nominal repeat; W=20 8s8p 15/16;
  mean 0.470 mV; all signed errors positive; leftover +0.479 mV.
- **Supported:** leftover is the analog floor; `tt_mm` tails are
  now ~0.23 mV rms.
- **Projected:** leftover diagnosis will show geometry and/or voltco
  versus ideal R, not AGAUSS.
- **Unknown:** yield; silicon; whether leftover scales with |z|;
  OPA189 + this array.
- **Contradicted:** “more documented area removes leftover”;
  unre-sized miller family (still closed).

## 11. Next dominant bottleneck

**Primary:** deterministic xhigh_po leftover (~+0.48 mV on vec 19)
occupying half the 1 mV high-side budget.

**Secondary:** residual `tt_mm` tails; Track B TIA; Network F insertion.
